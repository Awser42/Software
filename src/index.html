Browser and background work

Show Chromium only when you need to sign in or complete verification. Hiding the browser keeps the current login and queue active.

Product exports

Each ZIP contains a formatted product.xlsx and product.csv with Title, Variation, Price, Size, Color, Description, Seller Description, Features, Specifications and Images, plus an Images/ folder of JPEG files. The visible AliExpress AI overview is preferred; the lower seller description is the fallback. Missing fields stay blank and warnings appear in the app.

Settling time applies after each variation selection. More variations take longer; increase this time if prices update slowly.

Login cookies stay in the app’s Chromium profile under your Windows user account. To sign out, use AliExpress inside Chromium. This app does not export your cookies or password.
